## Why I built it

My father has sold used cars in Čepin, near Osijek, since 2014. I grew up around the lot and have worked in the business since 2021. What I saw was the same facts about the same car being typed again and again: into a spreadsheet, into each ad portal's form, onto a sheet of paper for the windscreen, and once more into a reply to a buyer.

As a computing student I could see that coding agents had become good enough to build real software. I had a business that needed it and I knew exactly what it should do.

## The public side

A buyer gets a catalogue that loads fast and answers the questions people actually ask before they drive out to look at a car.

- Search and filters, side-by-side comparison and saved cars.
- A page per car with full specification, photographs and the price.
- A trade-in form, a financing calculator and an import-cost calculator.
- Structured data for search engines and pages rendered ahead of time.

## The back office

Behind a login, one vehicle record feeds everything a dealer does with a car.

- **Inquiries and trade-ins** arrive as records with a response-time target and a push notification, not as emails to lose.
- **Offers** are numbered PDFs with a frozen copy of the car's data at the moment they were issued.
- **Window stickers** print from the record, with a QR code back to the car's page.
- **Ad portals** receive feeds built to each one's specification: Njuškalo, Index Oglasi, Čačkalo, and the Facebook and Google catalogues.
- **An assistant**, in its first version, answered questions about stock and prepared changes for confirmation. It grew into [the one in Kokpit](/work/assistant/).

## Cutting a car out of its background, in the browser

Before Showroom there was a simpler tool: remove the background of a car photograph without sending the photograph anywhere. It runs a 44 MB segmentation model inside the browser through WebAssembly, followed by my own clean-up of the mask for the ground line and the shadow under the car.

I chose the model by measuring, not by reputation. Against reference masks, the one I kept raised agreement along the edges of the car from 0.874 to 0.974 and cut disagreement with the reference from 12.6% to 2.6%.

## What it taught me

- **Where the boundary goes.** Public pages and staff workflows are separate applications in all but name. Deciding what each role can see came before any screen.
- **A model's output is a candidate.** Extracted data and drafted replies are reviewed by a person before they become a record or a sent message.
- **What I cannot claim.** I have no measured figure for time saved or cars sold faster, so there is none on this page. The feeds are built to each portal's specification; whether a portal ingests them depends on onboarding with that portal.

The system is also the subject of my undergraduate thesis at FERIT Osijek.
